How the research works

Research never stops. Every launch that passes your numbers gets a RugCheck report, its own token metadata (description and links) and the X post or account it links to, all within a few seconds. Launches that are already busy but still younger than your minimum age are researched early, so they alert the moment they're old enough.

The narrative score runs from 0 to 100: a real description (+20, short ones +10), its own X account (+15) and the X post it was made about (+15, or +20 with both; old or reused posts count less), a website (+8), Telegram (+5), a narrative match (+20 to +35), Grok seeing X posts about the token (+10), and a paid DexScreener profile or boost (+10). The strongest match is a narrative Grok found on X right now; after that come topics that the X posts of several new launches share, your own keywords, and tokens trending on Solana.

60+ reads as a strong narrative, 40 to 59 as some narrative. Only launches at or above your minimum (default 50) that also pass RugCheck alert. A narrative match can also mean a copycat of a coin that already ran, so open the X post before you act. Held launches are scored again on their own when new narratives arrive.

Data: GeckoTerminal (new pools, pool numbers, trending, chart candles), DexScreener (profiles, boosts, prices), Jupiter (the movers' top lists and stats), RugCheck (safety reports and the movers' rug scans), X's public embed service (linked posts), IPFS gateways (token metadata), Grok's X search (only with your xAI key) and a public Solana RPC (wallet activity, plus the movers' live prices and buy counts over its WebSocket). All requests only read data; your xAI key is sent only to api.x.ai. Keep one radar tab open: two tabs share the same free limits.

How movers are picked

Live prices: every token on the lists (up to 100) has its pool watched on-chain over your Solana RPC's WebSocket, so a trade moves its row within about a second (pump.fun curve, PumpSwap, Raydium, Orca and Meteora pools), and every number on screen is redrawn at least once a second. Rows marked live are streaming; the few pools the stream can't read get a fresh DexScreener price every second instead. Each list scrolls and shows up to 50 tokens; the order holds still while your pointer is on a list, and the numbers keep ticking. Click a row for its live chart.

Buy counts: B / S is buys and sells over the last 5 minutes. While a token streams, every buy the chain sees adds to it the moment it lands (exact per transaction on pump.fun and PumpSwap pools, one per pool change elsewhere), with the APIs' count covering the minutes before the radar started watching; buys/min is the last 60 seconds. Hover a count for its source.

Rising now: up at least 8% over the last 5 minutes (from the live price, Jupiter, GeckoTerminal or DexScreener), not down on the hour, more buys than sells, and enough trades and liquidity to trust the move. Ranked by the 5-minute gain, the last minute's move, buy pressure, volume pickup and the hourly trend.

Dip watch: ran at least 40% within the last hour, then pulled back 15 to 65% from the high with its liquidity still in place, and shows at least one bounce sign: back up 4%+ off the low, buys outnumbering sells over 5 minutes, or the last couple of minutes turning up. Tokens RugCheck or Jupiter flags are left out.

Rug scans: every token on the lists (and the next 100 candidates below them) is checked three ways, fastest first. Instantly, its mint and freeze authority (and Token-2022 traps such as a permanent delegate or a frozen-by-default account) are read straight from the chain, ten tokens per call, so MINT ON, FREEZE ON or AUTH ✓ shows within a second or two. Quickly, RugCheck's summary (score and risks) gives each row its verdict, up to 24 scans at once at a pace that starts at 8 calls a second and climbs while RugCheck keeps up. In full, the chart's token, launches, wallet trades and the top 10 rows of each list get the full report (dev holding, top-10 holders, insiders). The chart and the top rows are refreshed every 3 minutes, the rest every 10, and reports are kept between visits for 45 minutes, so the lists come back already scanned. One verdict per token is shared by the lists, the chart, launch research and wallet trades, so nothing is read twice. Tag colours mean the same thing in every theme: RUG ✓ lit green passes cleanly (full report, very low score), RUG ✓ green passes, RUG ⚠ orange passes with a raised score, RUG ✕ red fails your filters (the tags after it say why), RUGGED was pulled. Hover the badge for everything RugCheck found, or open the chart for the full scan. Failing tokens rank lower in Rising now and never show in Dip watch; "Hide risky" removes them.

The pool: Jupiter's top trending, traded and organic tokens, every launch the radar watches, Solana's trending pools of any age, verified launches and tokens your wallets trade. HELD means the radar held it back as a launch. Click any row for its live chart.

These are signs, not predictions: many dips keep dipping and many risers reverse, so check the chart and RugCheck first. Thresholds and the rug filter are in Filters; alerts and the live stream are in Settings.

    Trading

    Buy and sell from the launch cards and charts through your own Solana wallet. Swaps route through Jupiter; your wallet shows each one and asks you to approve it. Da Trading Tool never sees your keys or seed phrase.

    Connect with the Connect wallet button at the top. Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and other Solana wallets that run in your browser work. Opened as a file in Chrome, a wallet extension needs "Allow access to file URLs" turned on (chrome://extensions, then the wallet's Details). In the desktop and phone apps, Buy opens Jupiter in your browser or wallet app instead.
